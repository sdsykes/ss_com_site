🏎️ Go Karting

📍 Gran Karting Club, Bahía Feliz (south of the island)

Helmet on, foot down — vroom! Race around real twisty tracks and see who can do the fastest lap. There are smaller, slower karts for younger drivers and double karts so a grown-up can ride along with little ones.

  • 🏁 Racing has been happening here since 1983!
  • 👟 Wear closed shoes and tie long hair back.
🔗 Visit website
2

🏖️ Amadores Beach

📍 Between Puerto Rico and Tauro, Mogán (south-west)

A beach that looks like the Caribbean! Soft white-golden sand and calm, turquoise water that is sheltered from big waves by two rocky arms — perfect for paddling, splashing and building sandcastles.

  • 🌊 The water is usually calm, great for little swimmers.
  • 🧴 It's very sunny here — bring a hat and suncream!
🔗 Find out more
3

🦀 Rock Pools

📍 All around the coast — try Agaete, Bañaderos or La Laja

Become a sea explorer! Gran Canaria's black volcanic rocks make amazing natural pools that fill up with seawater. Peek in to spot crabs scuttling, tiny fish darting about and sea anemones waving.

  • 🔍 Take a bucket and look — but put creatures back gently.
  • ⚠️ Rocks can be slippery, and always check the waves with a grown-up.
🔗 Natural pools guide
4

💦 Aqualand

📍 Maspalomas (south)

The biggest splash on the island! Whizz down twisty water slides, float along the lazy river and play in the special kids' areas with little slides and water cannons. Warning: you WILL get wet! 😄

  • 📏 Some big slides have a height rule — measure yourself at the entrance!
  • 🩴 Bring flip-flops: the ground gets hot in the sun.
🔗 Visit website
5
🎄 Christmas only

🪐 Planeta Gran Canaria

📍 Infecar fairground, Las Palmas · late December to early January

A giant Christmas holiday festival just for kids and families! It's packed with bouncy castles, games, sports, workshops, shows and things to make and discover — a whole planet of fun under one roof.

  • 🗓️ It only opens during the Christmas holidays — check the dates each year.
  • 🎁 Around 35,000 visitors came last time!
🔗 Visit website
6

🤿 Las Canteras

📍 Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (north)

A super long golden beach right in the city! A natural rock reef called "La Barra" stops the big waves, so the water is calm — brilliant for snorkelling and spotting colourful fish. At low tide you can almost walk out to the reef!

  • 🐠 Hundreds of kinds of sea creatures live around La Barra.
  • 🍦 Walk along the promenade for an ice cream afterwards.
🔗 Find out more
7
🎄 Christmas only

🏰 Sand Sculptures on Las Canteras

📍 La Puntilla end of Las Canteras beach · mid-December to early January

The biggest sandcastle you've ever seen! Every Christmas, sand artists from around the world carve a giant nativity scene out of the beach — with people, animals and buildings up to 5 metres tall. It's all made from sand and water!

  • 🏗️ Around 2,000 tonnes of sand are used — that's heavier than 300 elephants!
  • 💝 Money raised goes to help charities.
🔗 Visit website
8

🦈 Poema del Mar

📍 By the port in Las Palmas (north)

Dive into an underwater world without getting wet! This amazing aquarium has glowing jellyfish, sharks, rays, funny-looking fish and a giant curved window into the deep blue ocean that feels like standing on the sea floor.

  • 🪼 Watch the jellyfish — they glow and drift like space aliens.
  • 🌧️ A great idea for a cloudy or rainy day.
🔗 Visit website
9

🎡 Holiday World

📍 Maspalomas (south)

A funfair full of flashing lights! Ride the big ferris wheel, spin on the rides, try the bumper cars and play in the arcade. It's extra magical in the evening when everything lights up.

  • 🎢 Over 30 rides for all ages.
  • 🌙 Check the opening hours — it's open into the evening.
🔗 Visit website
10

🎳 Las Arenas Arcade & Bowling

📍 Las Arenas shopping centre, Las Palmas (north)

Strike! Knock down all ten pins at the bowling lanes (there are bumpers to help beginners), then win tickets on the arcade games and swap them for prizes. Great fun for the whole family on any day.

  • 🎟️ Collect tickets from the games to trade for prizes.
  • 🧦 Bowling shoes are needed — bring socks!
🔗 Visit website
