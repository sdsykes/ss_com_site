Boat trip

Dolphin watching

From the south coast harbours

The boat trips are quite nice, and you'll almost certainly see dolphins.

  • Some options
  • Canco → Whale & dolphin watching from Puerto de Mogán
  • Glass Bottom Boat → Dolphin search, plus a visit to Mogán market
  • Multiacuatic → Dolphin & whale safari from Puerto Rico
Day trip

Visit Agaete

The north-west coast

A pretty little town with nice restaurants on the sea front. You can swim there too.

For lunch, try Las Nasas — a good fish restaurant right on the harbour (see Where to eat).
Find out more →
Caves

Cenobio de Valerón

Santa María de Guía, the north coast

A honeycomb of hundreds of little caves carved into a cliff by the ancient Canarians and used to store their grain. Easy to combine with a trip to Agaete.

Visit the caves →

Vegueta

Las Palmas

The historic old town — cobbled streets, colonial houses, the cathedral and the Casa de Colón. Lovely for a stroll.
