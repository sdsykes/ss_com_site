Scribblitz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Scribblitz is a drawing game for iPhone and iPad made by Stephen Sykes. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

What we collect

Nothing. Scribblitz has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We, the developer, don't collect or keep any information about you or your drawings.

What stays on your device

  • Your player name is stored on your device so you don't have to type it again.
  • The AI that guesses your drawings is Apple Intelligence, running on your own device. Your drawings aren't sent to us or to anyone else for this.

What's shared with other players

When you play a multiplayer game, your device shares only what the game needs with the other players in it:

  • your player name,
  • what the AI guesses while you draw,
  • your drawing at the end of each round, so everyone's drawings can be shown on the results screen.

This information goes only to the devices of the people in your game. It isn't stored after the game ends.

Playing nearby

When you play with people nearby, your device connects directly to theirs over your local network (Wi-Fi). Nothing goes over the internet.

Playing over the internet

When you host a game with an invite code, or join one with a code, the game information listed above passes through a relay server we run on Cloudflare. The relay only passes messages between the players in your game. It doesn't read them, store them or keep any record of who played. A game's code stops working as soon as its host leaves.

Like any internet service, Cloudflare handles your device's IP address so it can deliver the messages. We don't keep it or use it for anything else.

Local network permission

Scribblitz asks for permission to use the local network. This is only used to find nearby games and connect to the other players' devices. You don't need it to play over the internet with a code.

Children

Scribblitz doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
